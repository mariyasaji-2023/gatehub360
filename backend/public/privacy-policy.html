Privacy Policy for GateHub360

Last updated: July 29, 2026

GateHub360 ("we", "our", "the app") is a society, property, and hostel management app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and who we share it with.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and profile photo, collected when you sign in (via Google Sign-In or email/phone authentication through Firebase).
  • Role and residency information: your role in the app (resident, apartment association, property owner, hostel owner, tenant, or service provider), and the society, flat, property, or hostel you're associated with.
  • Enquiries and communication: messages, enquiries, or contact details you submit when enquiring about a listed property or hostel.
  • Push notification token: a device identifier (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) used to deliver notifications to your device.
  • Payment information: if you make a payment in the app, it is processed directly by Razorpay, our payment gateway. We do not receive or store your card, UPI, or bank account details — we only receive a transaction reference to confirm payment success.

How we use this information

  • To create and manage your account and verify your identity
  • To connect residents, owners, tenants, and service providers within a society or property
  • To process and confirm payments
  • To send you notifications relevant to your account (e.g. notices, enquiries, payment confirmations)
  • To operate and improve the app

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers that help us run the app:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and push notifications
  • Razorpay — payment processing
  • Other users within your society/property, to the extent necessary for the app to function (e.g. an owner sees enquiries from interested tenants)

Data storage

Your account and app data is stored on our servers. We retain your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below.

Children's privacy

GateHub360 is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at hub360company@gmail.com.
